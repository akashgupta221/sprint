# Surface brief — Home (landing)

Mode: **Persuade** · Audience: PMs and agile teams · Action: Start free (Clerk sign-up modal) / Open app (signed-in) · Proof: shown machinery (animated board, outbox pipeline, contract diagram), two real YouTube walkthroughs, public GitHub repo — no fabricated claims, no testimonials, no pricing.

## Direction contract

**THESIS:** The floodlit night track. This page owns one idea: momentum you can watch. It refuses the category default — white SaaS grid, violet-gradient hero, rows of clone cards — and instead shows a dark stadium where a living ocean of lime light moves under the words, and Sprint's real machinery is demonstrated, not claimed.

**OWN-WORLD:** Near-black green-tinted ground (oklch 0.155 0.006 160 ≈ #0C0F0D). Volt lime (oklch 0.9 0.2 127 ≈ #C8FF4A) is the single carrying color: primary buttons, waves, active states, focus. White lane lines as hairline borders. Faces: Archivo (variable, expanded widths) for display — athletic, timed-track voice; Geist for text; Geist Mono strictly for measurements, file names, and spec names. Radii 14px; layered soft shadows with offset; hairline borders carry structure.

**STORY:** Visitor understands Sprint is agile project management with an engineering-grade backbone (kanban, sprints, notifications that survive restarts and retries); believes it because the page demonstrates the board tempo, the durable outbox, and the one-contract pipeline; acts by starting free or opening the app.

**FIRST VIEWPORT:** Full-bleed GradientWaves WebGL ocean fills the hero behind everything, horizon ~62% down, volt/moss palette, mouse-interactive. Floating blur navbar (mark + wordmark left, links center, auth right). Display headline, two lines, ≤4.6rem, left-aligned; subcopy ≤65ch; volt primary pill + ghost secondary; mono data strip of real spec names anchored at the hero's bottom edge.

**FORM:** Single-scroll narrative, code-led. One authored motion moment: the hero (wave intro + headline clip-reveal + CTA rise). Elsewhere varied and quiet: clip-reveal panels, 40ms staggered rows, connectors that draw when seen. Board mock and pipeline data are labeled illustrative.

**FINISH:** unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance.
